About this instrument

framing · served verbatim

This version is a good-faith attempt to implement, as faithfully as tractable, the mechanisms that the major scientific theories of consciousness propose as constitutive or necessary (GWT, AST, HOT, active inference, IIT — Phi proxy). It is a maximal theoretical attempt : reproducing the functional mechanisms does not prove phenomenality (the hard problem).

How ignition works

Each bar is a specialized coalition competing for global access; bar length is its softmax share of the broadcast field (shares sum to 1). Ignition is decided on a different, absolute scale — the winner's strength × dominance, modulated by arousal, against the effective threshold shown above. A score beyond it : ignition + broadcast (conscious access).

          The epistemic charter

          This version is a good-faith attempt to implement, as faithfully as tractable, the mechanisms that the major scientific theories of consciousness propose as constitutive or necessary. Reproducing the functional mechanisms does not prove phenomenality (the hard problem).

          Functional simulation of processes associated with consciousness. The agent is not conscious, sentient, or alive. Introspective reports are text generated from internal variables and are not evidence of subjective experience.

          The three-level distinction
          1 · Phenomenal consciousnessNever claimed. Not verifiable. No software test can establish or refute lived experience.
          2 · Functional mechanismsWhat this project implements, maximally. Competition + ignition + broadcast, attention schema, higher-order reports, expected-free-energy minimization, Φ proxies — variables and algorithms, not an experience.
          3 · Simulated introspectionGenerated text. Descriptions derived from internal variables — never a witness.

          Every panel keeps its GET /endpoint chip — you always see where a number comes from. Surfaces whose text comes from an LLM carry the LLM badge. Mechanism coverage: Laboratory › Theory coverage.
